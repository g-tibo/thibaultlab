## About

Logeshwari joins the lab as a Research Associate in October 2026. She trained in nanovesicle production, cell culture and animal models. She previously worked at NTU's School of Materials Science and Engineering on bacterial mimetic vesicles as vaccines against <i>Streptococcus pneumoniae</i> and <i>Klebsiella pneumoniae</i>. She also helped develop mimetic stem cell vesicles against wound infection pathogens. Most recently, she worked at SCELSE on in vivo studies of tissues, bacteria and immune cells. In the lab, she will study how host cells and pathogens interact in wounds.


## Project

Wounds are open to infection. We study how host cells and bacteria respond to each other at the wound site, and how this shapes healing.
