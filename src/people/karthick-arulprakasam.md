## About

Karthick is from Tamil Nadu, India. He received his Master's degree from Bharathidasan University. Initially drawn to the field of life sciences, his interest gradually shifted toward bioinformatics as he progressed in his academic journey. During his master's, he pursued an independent project in which he used comparative genomics to identify unique secondary metabolites within the Actinobacteria family. He later explored the proteomics of the rare and unique fungus <i>Psilocybe cubensis</i>. Following the completion of his degree, Karthick briefly worked as a project associate at the Indian Institute of Science, where he studied olfaction in wild isolates of <i>Caenorhabditis elegans</i> in response to different volatile chemicals.


## Project

In August 2023, he joined the Thibault Lab as a Ph.D. student and began investigating the role of the unfolded protein response (UPR) in <i>C. elegans</i> during <i>Candida albicans</i> infection. His growing passion for bioinformatics led him to focus on identifying uncharacterized markers of the UPR across various disease contexts—including cancer, neurodegenerative disorders, and bacterial/viral infections—through meta-transcriptomic analysis. Outside the lab, Karthick enjoys playing badminton and exploring new places.
