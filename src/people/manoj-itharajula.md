## About

Embarking on my PhD odyssey, I delve into the mesmerizing confluence of Large Language Models (LLMs) and literature on human gene functions, aiming to unravel the enigmatic lexicon of genes through sophisticated knowledge graphs. Driven by an insatiable curiosity and fervent passion, I aspire to illuminate the intricate symphony of our genetic code, harnessing the transformative prowess of AI to transcend our understanding of gene functions.
